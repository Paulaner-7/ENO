# Takedown requests

SCANCORE is a reader and player. It does not host, store or distribute any
comics, novels or video and runs no extension repository. What people read
is fetched from the sites they choose by their own device. Built-in support
for some public sites can be switched off in every installed copy at once,
without a release, and removed from the code on request.

So there is usually nothing in this repository or in SCANCORE's releases to
take down. If you hold rights to a work and find it on a site, the site that
hosts it, or the repository that publishes an extension for that site, is
who can remove it.

## When something in this project is the problem

If you believe this repository, a SCANCORE release or scancore.onl itself
contains or links to material that infringes your rights, write to
**logesh@mediumdev.com** with:

1. who you are and the work you hold rights to;
2. the exact address (repository file, release asset or page) of the material;
3. a statement that you believe in good faith that its use is not authorised
   by you, your agent or the law, and that the information you give is
   accurate.

We answer within a few working days and remove what is ours to remove.
Content that users post on scancore.onl (comments and reviews) is handled the
same way.
